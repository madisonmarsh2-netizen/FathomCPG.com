Privacy policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Fathom CPG (“Fathom,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit fathomcpg.com, submit information through our website, or otherwise communicate with us.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Company or brand name
  • Website or social media information
  • Information about your brand, business, retail presence, team, goals, timeline, or project needs
  • Any other information you choose to provide through our forms, email, or other communications

You are not required to provide personal information simply to browse our website.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as your:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring website or source
  • Pages viewed
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Date and time of visits
  • General interactions with the website

This information may be collected through cookies and similar technologies used to operate and understand the website.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Review information submitted through our Partner With Me form
  • Determine whether Fathom may be a good fit for your business or project
  • Communicate with you about potential or existing projects
  • Provide and improve our services
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • Understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our website
  • Keep appropriate business records
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Submitting information through our website does not create a client, consulting, or other professional relationship between you and Fathom CPG.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Fathom may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website function properly and understand how visitors interact with it.

We may use third-party service providers for website hosting, analytics, forms, scheduling, email, or other business functions. These providers may process certain information in connection with the services they provide to us.

You can typically control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of portions of the website.

How We Share Information

Fathom CPG does not sell your personal information for money.

We may share information with trusted service providers that assist us in operating our website and business, such as website hosting, analytics, email, scheduling, form-processing, or other technology providers.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or a valid legal request
  • Enforce our agreements or protect our legal rights
  • Detect or prevent fraud, security issues, or misuse
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Fathom CPG, our clients, website visitors, or others

If Fathom CPG is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, information may also be transferred as part of that transaction as permitted by law.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect the information we maintain.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or method of data storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use appropriate care when submitting information online.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services.

Fathom CPG does not control these third parties and is not responsible for their privacy, security, or data-handling practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites or services you choose to use.

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, subject to applicable law and any information we are legally permitted or required to retain.

If you receive marketing communications from Fathom, you may opt out at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions included in the communication or by contacting us directly.

Depending on where you live and the laws applicable to Fathom CPG, you may have additional rights concerning your personal information.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us at madison@fathomcpg.com.

California and Other State Privacy Rights

Residents of certain U.S. states may have additional rights under applicable state privacy laws.

Where such laws apply to Fathom CPG, eligible individuals may have rights that include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information and receiving information about how their personal information is collected, used, or disclosed.

We will respond to applicable privacy requests in accordance with legal requirements.

Children’s Privacy

Fathom CPG provides business-to-business services, and our website is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our website, business practices, services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be revised.
