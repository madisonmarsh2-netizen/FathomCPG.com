Methodology

Three steps to fathom your numbers.

Get the data in, get to the bottom of it, turn what surfaces into a story that wins the room. The order matters: storytelling without the analytics falls apart under one question, analytics without the story is a spreadsheet nobody opens.

Good brands

01

Start with what's true.

Every engagement starts the same way: pull together everything that's already there, then find out what it's actually saying, not the story everyone's been assuming.

02

Find your growth story.

It's buried in the data: the real driver of growth, the cost that's quietly adding up, and the thing the market's been saying that hasn't been heard yet. This is the part that gets skipped when there's no time for it, and it's the part that matters most.

03

Make the case.

A deck, a diagnostic, a live tracker, a recommendation. Built around the specific thing you need to prove, not a generic template.
