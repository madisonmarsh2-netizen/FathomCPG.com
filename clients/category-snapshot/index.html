The situation

Brand X grew +42.0% while the category grew +17.5%.

At Retailer X, Brand X is the #2 brand in the category over the last 26 weeks, and it gained 2.5 points of share in a year.

The job was to find where that growth comes from and turn it into an ask a buyer can say yes to.

01

Brand X adds the most dollar growth of the top 10 brands.

It's the #1 contributor to category growth, and every existing item is growing year over year in dollars and units. Brand X has 4 of the top 10 items in the category.

It also sells faster per store than any other brand: $36 per item, per store, per week, a 22% lead over the next closest competitor.

02

3 of the top 4 new items in the category are Brand X's.

Brand X's new items bring in 36.6% of all new item dollars across the 23 new items in the category.

They lead on dollars per store, and price does most of the work. They sell at about 2x the average price of other new items.

The watch-out: Brand J's Item 1 sells more units per store than any Brand X new item, at about half the price.

03

Items 4 and 5 lost stores and still led the growth.

Both items lost about 25 stores year over year. Even so, together they drive over half of the growth from continuing items.

That made them the clearest opportunity at Retailer X.

04 · The ask

Bring Items 4 and 5 to all 135 stores.

Both lost about 25 stores year over year: 88 vs. 114, and 89 vs. 113. Even so, they drive over half of the growth from continuing items.

What Brand X should do next.

  • Push Retailer X to bring Items 4 and 5 to all 135 stores.
  • Invest in promotion, retail media and displays to keep Item 1 on top as growth slows.
  • Watch Items 2 and 3 to confirm growth isn't just new stores.
