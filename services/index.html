Everything I do falls into four areas. Each one can be a one-time project or a monthly retainer, scoped to your brand, your category and the data you already have.

The four pillars

Each one works as a one-time project or a monthly retainer. Most brands start with one and grow into the others.

01

Data storytelling

Retailer review meetings matter, a good one can change the trajectory of your company. You've already put in the hard work building a product that deserves to be there. I turn that hard work into the specific insights a buyer needs to see, backed by category data they already trust.

  • Retailer and line-review decks
  • Internal leadership readouts
  • New Item Submissions
02

Data Engine and dashboards

Messy portal data, broker reports and spreadsheets turned into one clean dashboard a founder can actually check every week. Velocity, distribution, and consumption insights for all your key retailers.

  • Weekly trends tracker by retailer and SKU
  • Retailer portal, distributor and syndicated data in one place
  • Monday morning readout
03

PPP analysis

Profitability, promo, performance. A full read of where your trade dollars go, which promotions pay for themselves and which never will, and a calendar built around the winners.

  • Trade spend diagnostic
  • Post-promo reads and lift versus cost
  • Promo calendar built for your brand
04

Market research

The category, the competition and the consumer, pulled into a short read you can act on. Reviews, surveys, social listening and customer feedback turned into real product and claim decisions.

  • Category and whitespace snapshot
  • Consumer feedback synthesis
  • Competitive set and pricing read

Questions I get

The honest answers, before you have to ask.

Do I need syndicated data to work with you?+
No. Plenty of brands start with a retailer portal export and their own sales file. If SPINS or Nielsen would change the answer, I'll say so and help you decide whether it's worth the cost.
What if I just need one project?+
That works. One project, a retainer, whatever fits your brand right now, there's no lock-in either way. If you move to a retainer down the line, it runs month to month with 30 days' notice, and the dashboard and the work stay yours.
What happens if I need to pause?+
Retainers run month to month with 30 days' notice. Pause, downgrade, or stop. The dashboard and the work stay yours.
Can you work with my broker or distributor?+
Yes, and I usually prefer to. I'll reconcile their reports with what you're seeing so everyone is looking at the same number in the same meeting.
Is Fathom right for a brand that hasn't launched yet?+
Probably not yet. Fathom is built for brands past launch and in real retail. Once you have a few months of sell-through, the Market research snapshot is the right door.
