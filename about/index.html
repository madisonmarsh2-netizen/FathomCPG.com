Mission

Fathom CPG is the fractional analytics and storytelling partner that helps founder-led CPG brands fathom their own numbers, real data, real insights, at a fraction of what it costs to hire a full team, so every brand walks into the room ready to win.

The origin story

01Before

Founder-led brands are drowning in data but starving for insight. I saw it firsthand over three-plus years inside CPG startups, the 9th hire at Seven Sundays, then the 8th hire at GoNanas, where I became the entire analytics function as the company's sole Sales Coordinator. Hands-on with syndicated data, trade planning, and category management, building the retailer decks that made the case for shelf space at both natural and conventional national retailers.

02The gap

Running that function myself, I saw how much founder-led brands need hard data to prove their incrementality and value to the retailer, and how rarely they have the time or money to get it done. Data is messy, and pulling it together takes real time. On a small team, there's always something more urgent, so the numbers take a back seat. Most brands end up making real decisions without the proof to back up what they already know is true.

03Fathom

I started Fathom to bring that same depth to brands who don't have someone in that seat yet. Real, hands-on CPG experience, not theory, built for your brand and customized to meet your category and data needs. Every founder deserves to understand their own numbers, it's the best proof of what's working, and the clearest way to keep it growing.

04Now

Working with founder-led brands from $0 to $50M that are past launch, in real retail, and ready to actually understand what their numbers are telling them.

My story
