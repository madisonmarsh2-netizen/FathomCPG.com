Mission

Fractional analytics and storytelling for founder‑led CPG brands.

The origin story

01Before

Founder-led brands are drowning in data but starving for insight. I saw it firsthand as the 9th hire at Seven Sundays, then the 8th hire at GoNanas, where I became the entire analytics function. I built the retailer decks that made the case for shelf space at natural and conventional national retailers.

02The gap

Running that function myself, I saw how badly founder-led brands need hard data, and how rarely they have the time or money to get it. On a small team, something's always more urgent, so the numbers take a back seat. Most brands end up making real decisions without the proof to back them up.

03Fathom

I started Fathom to bring that same depth to brands who don't have someone in that seat yet. Real, hands-on CPG experience, built around your category and your data. Every founder deserves to understand their own numbers. It's the best proof of what's working.

04Now

Today I work with founder-led brands from $0 to $50M, past launch and already in real retail. Brands that want a real partner in their numbers, not another report to skim. Together, we turn the data you're already collecting into the proof that wins the next shelf.

My story

I came up on the side of the table where the data lives.

I learned what a buyer actually reads on a slide and what they skip. I learned where trade dollars go to disappear. And I learned that most founders don't lack ideas, they lack the hard data to back them.

My job is to make sure you walk into the room with great ideas backed by data.
